Packing problems

Fitting shapes into as little space as possible. A hobby and a testbed for mixed discrete–continuous optimization. The 21 November 2026 ledger records 41 circle packings above their linked public comparison values. Of these, 30 improve the radius by at least one part per million and 11 are precision leads. Earlier arrangements appear below the current candidates.

Squares: minimize the container side. Circles: maximize the common radius in the indicated container.
