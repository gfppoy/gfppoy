Current Work

Neurosymbolic robot control
Robots typically run hand-engineered controllers that struggle in situations their designers didn’t anticipate. I’m developing methods that refine existing controllers, such as behaviour trees, with learned adaptations. The robot improves from experience while keeping an explicit decision structure that engineers can inspect and formally verify.
Logic gate network optimization
Logic gate networks are compact models that run very efficiently on hardware. Their discrete structure makes training difficult: improvements in a continuous relaxation need not translate into better discrete circuits. I’m developing methods for learning useful features and optimizing these circuits, with the aim of making logic a practical substrate for machine learning.
Implicit bias in reinforcement learning
When many policies are equally good under the training objective, the optimizer’s implicit preferences determine which one is learned, and with it how the policy generalizes. I’m developing theory that characterizes this implicit bias for policy-gradient methods.
